## Planetary orbit photos

- Earth asset: `public/images/earth-globe.webp`. Source: [Earth from Space](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-from-space-885/). True-color globe using Terra MODIS land-surface data and NOAA GOES cloud data. Credit: Reto Stöckli, Nazmi El Saleous, and Marit Jentoft-Nilsen, NASA GSFC.
- Moon asset: `public/images/moon-globe.webp`. Source: [Full Moon on Christmas Day](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/rare-full-moon-on-christmas-day/). Created from NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter images. Credit: NASA/Goddard/Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter.
- Mars asset: `public/images/mars-globe.webp`. Source: [Mars Planet Globe](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mars-planet-globe/). Valles Marineris hemisphere mosaic assembled from 102 Viking Orbiter images. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech.
